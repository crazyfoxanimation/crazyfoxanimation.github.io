My Security Door are Melbourne's most trusted specialists in Crimsafe and steel security doors. As part of their rebrand, I created a Virtual Showroom to showcase their full range of door designs.

The challenge was building something that could replace the need for a physical showroom, letting customers browse, compare and visualise different door styles in a realistic home environment without leaving their couch.

Detailed 3D modelling and rendering of each door design was combined with a fully interactive virtual tour, giving the client a sales tool that works around the clock.

Approach
High-fidelity 3D renders of every door style, built into an interactive virtual tour platform for an immersive browsing experience.
Outcome
A 24/7 sales asset that lets customers explore the full product range in a realistic setting, reducing reliance on a physical showroom.
Tools
Blender (Cycles), Virtual Tour Platform, Adobe Creative Suite
