The brief required bold, high-impact 3D visuals that would translate the energy of Formula 1 into an immersive bar environment, merging the Jack Daniel's brand identity with the precision and speed of McLaren's racing world.

Concept-led design through to final 3D animation, balancing brand guidelines from two major clients simultaneously.
