Mixed Media Storytelling Series
Deliverable
Live Action / 3D Animation Blend
Roles
3D Modelling 3D Animation Editing

Created a series of stories that mix live action with 3D animation for the Brewarrina Aboriginal Cultural Museum.

This project required a sensitive and considered approach, using 3D animation to bring ancient stories and creatures to life in a way that respects and honours the cultural significance of the subject matter.

The blend of live action footage with 3D elements creates an immersive experience that connects museum visitors with the deep history of the region.

Approach
Seamlessly compositing 3D animated elements into live action footage to create a cohesive, culturally respectful visual narrative.
Highlight
Recreating the Diprotodon, a giant prehistoric marsupial, as a fully rigged and animated 3D character.
Tools
Blender (Cycles), After Effects, DaVinci Resolve
